```
**Here's what's been brewing** ☕

Three updates since the last post. Store installs pick them up on their
own; Setup.exe folks are already current.

**Your phone can buzz now** (new in 1.25)
Settings > Alerts can route any alert to your phone through Pushover —
the app a bunch of you already use — or ntfy (free, no account). An alt
drops out overnight and your phone knows, even with Discord closed. You
can also get an "all good — 6 accounts in" mark every two hours while
things run, so silence itself becomes the warning. One-time setup, and
the Test button proves it works before you trust it.

**Roblox stays windowed** (1.25)
A crash or a stray Alt+Enter used to leave your next launch swallowing
the whole screen. RoRoRo now clears that saved fullscreen flag before
every launch. On by default.

**Store installs caught up** (1.24)
"Start RoRoRo with Windows" and Discord Join now work on Store installs
— both had quietly never worked there. Direct downloads always had them.

**RoRoRo keeps score** (1.23)
History opens with your stats — the most alts you've ever run at once,
total hours, longest session — plus per-alt login streaks that only
count days the alt actually landed in a game. Race you.

The full feature list lives here now:
https://estevanhernandez-stack-ed.github.io/ROROROblox/
Setup.exe: https://github.com/estevanhernandez-stack-ed/ROROROblox/releases/latest
Something broken? Ping me or open an issue.
```
