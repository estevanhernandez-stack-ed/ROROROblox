# What's new in this version — v1.25.0.0

> Paste the fenced block into Partner Center → your app → **Store listings** → *What's new in this
> version*.
>
> **Written for someone on the v1.24 Store install.** The phone alerts are the headline; the
> uptime mark's missing-mark trick is the one mechanism worth teaching in this field because it
> is the feature's value, not its plumbing.

---

```
Your phone can buzz now
• Settings > Alerts has a new push section: route any alert to your
  phone through Pushover or ntfy. An alt drops out — your phone
  knows, even with Discord closed. One-time setup, and Test my
  phone proves it works before you trust it. Your keys stay
  encrypted on your PC; nothing is sent unless an alert fires, and
  then only to the service you set up.

Alerts go everywhere you tick
• Desktop, your Discord channel, the clan channel, your phone —
  tick any mix per alert instead of picking just one. Your old
  routing carried over.

Two new alerts, off until you turn them on
• A recycle finishing tells you how much memory it clawed back.
  Uptime marks say "4h up — 6 accounts in" every two hours while
  things run — so a mark that doesn't arrive means the PC or the
  app died, the one failure nothing can announce directly.

Roblox stays windowed
• If a crash or Alt+Enter left Roblox saved as fullscreen, RoRoRo
  turns that off before every launch. Toggle in Settings > Startup,
  on by default.

Rolling back?
• If you go back to an older version after setting up the new alert
  routing, re-set your routing there — older versions quietly skip
  options they don't know.
```

---

## What is deliberately not in this copy

- **DPAPI, notify.dat, topics-as-bearer-credentials, priority mappings.** "Your keys stay
  encrypted on your PC" is the same fact in this reader's language; the topic-secrecy coaching
  lives in the Settings copy at the moment it matters.
- **The flash fix and ghost defaults.** Real polish, but this field sells the version to someone
  deciding whether to care; a fixed flash is felt, not read about.
- **Which service to pick.** The Settings copy steers (iPhone → Pushover) at decision time.
