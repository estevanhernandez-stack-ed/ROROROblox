```
**RoRoRo v1.33 is out** 🔔

Store: it'll arrive on its own, or open the Store app and hit "Get updates."
Setup.exe folks: it updates itself as usual.

**Alerts now go only where you send them.**

If you ever unticked Desktop for memory warnings and kept getting them
popping up anyway — that was real, and it wasn't you misreading the table.
Memory warnings had their own way of reaching you that ignored your
choices entirely. Every alert goes through the same place now, so the
table, the per-account mute and the quiet period work the same for all of
them.

**You decide how often they repeat.** It was locked at five minutes. Now
it's yours, from "Every time" up to once an hour, and you can make one
kind chattier or quieter than the rest. Still five minutes if you don't
touch it.

**The pop-up is ours now.** Themed like the rest of the app instead of a
Windows balloon, readable over a fullscreen game, and if it names just one
account you can click it to jump straight to that account's row.

**Pick what it sounds like — and hear it first.** The RoRoRo chime, the
Windows default, or silence. There's a "Hear it" button right next to it,
because choosing between three sounds you can't hear until one happens to
fire isn't really choosing.

**Idle alerts are for accounts actually in a game.** An alt parked at the
Roblox home screen doesn't get one anymore. Roblox can't kick it out of
something it isn't in, so there was nothing to warn you about.

Two things worth knowing:

• If you set the sound to silence and still hear something, that's a
  plugin. Ur Score makes its own noise on its own alerts, and nothing in
  RoRoRo's settings reaches into it.
• If you've got Roblox presence privacy switched on for an account, it
  won't get idle alerts — we need to see that it's in a game, and that's
  exactly what privacy hides.

And there's something on the About page for you specifically. Click the
version number six or seven times. It's genuinely six or seven — the app
picks at random, so don't bother arguing with each other about it.

Download: https://github.com/estevanhernandez-stack-ed/ROROROblox/releases/latest
```
