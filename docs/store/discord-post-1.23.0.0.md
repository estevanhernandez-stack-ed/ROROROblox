# Clan Discord post — v1.23.0.0

> Post when the Store listing actually shows 1.23, not when certification clears — same rule as
> 1.21 and 1.22. Setup.exe users get it within a day of the GitHub release going live regardless.
>
> **Written for someone on v1.22.** Single release of news.
>
> **The stats section leads with the brag, not the feature.** "Most alts you've ever run at once"
> is a number this crowd will screenshot and compare. That's the post; everything else supports it.
>
> **The streak rule is stated plainly** because it will generate the one question people actually
> ask: "why is my streak 1?" Answer's in the post before they ask it — streaks need the account to
> LAND in a game, and they start counting from this version.
>
> **The connector fix gets two lines.** The handful running Claude with their alts know exactly
> what was broken; everyone else scrolls past. If the connector itself gets its own announcement
> later, remember its install instructions must name the launcher (the casing gotcha) — that's the
> connector post's job, not this one's.
>
> **What is deliberately NOT in here:** rollup architecture, DayKey/DST, the fold, register
> numbers, thread-affinity vocabulary. "A rare startup crash is fixed" is one line and done.

---

```
**RoRoRo v1.23 is out** 🎉

Store: it'll arrive on its own, or open the Store app and hit "Get updates."
Setup.exe folks: it updates itself as usual.

**RoRoRo keeps score now**

Open History and the top of the page is yours: the most alts you've ever
had running at once, total hours across all your accounts, your
most-played game, your longest session, and a leaderboard of your alts by
hours. It starts already filled in from your recent history.

Everything is computed on your PC and stays there — nothing gets sent
anywhere, same as always.

**Login streaks, per alt**

Every alt tracks its own streak of days it actually got INTO a game.
Launching doesn't count — an alt that got stuck at the home screen didn't
log in, and the streak knows the difference. One alt missing a day doesn't
break the others.

Streaks start counting from this version — the old history didn't record
what they need, so everyone starts even. Race you.

**If you renamed an alt**

New History entries now show your nickname instead of the account's old
Roblox name. Old entries keep whatever name they were written with.

**For the Claude crowd**

Telling Claude to stop your alts actually stops them now — clean close,
Roblox saves its own settings on the way out. If you don't know what this
means, ignore it.

Also fixed: the plugin install window no longer hides its own buttons on
long descriptions, and a rare crash that could stop the app from starting.

Setup.exe: <link to release>
Something broken? Ping me or open an issue.
```
