**RoRoRo v1.37 is out** 🎯

Store: quit RoRoRo first (right-click the tray icon, Quit), then let the
Store update it. Setup.exe folks: it updates itself as usual.

other out. It refreshes the one you have and tells you to add a different
account.

**One favour.** RoRoRo has thousands of installs and about twenty ratings
on the Store, and a couple of those are people guessing it's a virus. If
it's working for you, a rating does more than anything else to help the
next player trust it. Store installs get asked once, after a few good
days. Either button makes it go away for good.

Nothing else moved. Your accounts, themes, plugins and history carry over.
