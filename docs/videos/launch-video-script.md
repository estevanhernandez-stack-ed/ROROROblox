## Read this before typesetting anything

**Only three cards in this video carry motion-graphics text.** Everything from 0:09 to 0:40 is
the app's own interface in a screen recording, and that is a different localization surface:

| | 0:00–0:03 | 0:03–0:09 | 0:09–0:40 | 0:40–0:46 | 0:46–0:57 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **What's on screen** | Opening card | Roblox gameplay grid | RoRoRo UI (accounts, Squad Launch, History, Theme Builder) | Quality-of-life card | Closing / QR card |
| **Who owns the text** | You — card 1 below | no text | **The app** | You — card 2 below | You — card 3 below |

The middle section does not need translated captions from us. As of **v1.27 the whole app is
localized** into these six languages, so to localize that footage you re-record it with the app
in the target language — the UI follows the OS language automatically, or it can be pinned in
**Settings → Appearance → Language**. Retyping those strings as overlays would be wrong twice:
they would drift from the app, and the app already says them correctly.

## What stays English on purpose

Checked against the shipped resource files, not chosen by feel:

- **`Recycle`** and **`Squad Launch`** — product nouns. Both stay English in all six languages
  in the app itself, so the cards keep them too. A card that says "Recyclage" over a button that
  says "Recycle" reads as a translation error.
- **`Streamer mode`** — the opposite case: the app *does* translate it, so the cards use the
  app's exact wording per language (`Mode streamer`, `Streamer-Modus`, `Режим стримера`,
  `Modo streamer`, `Tryb streamera`, `Modo streamer`).
- **`RORORO`**, **`626 LABS`**, **`IMAGINE SOMETHING ELSE.`**, the URL, `Roblox`, `Windows`,
  `Mac`, `RAM`, `Microsoft Store` — brand and platform names.
- **`Ctrl`** becomes **`Strg`** in German only. That is the physical key on a German keyboard;
  every other language ships the key labelled `Ctrl`.

English. The six feature labels share one grid cell, so that cell was sized for `Keyboard

11-character `Auto-update` it replaces. Per-line percentages flag the wrong things in both
directions; slot width is what actually clips.
