RoRoRo v1.41: Roblox at a glance. C1 mocks

Every account, game and sentence of notice detail on this page is made up. The five notice sentences that are RoRoRo's own strings (spec §7.4) are exact; the contested, known-issue and drift texts are stand-ins for v1.40's strings and the feed's text. Colours are the app's own theme slots (the four built-ins and Flatline); use the switcher or ?theme=brand|midnight|magenta-heat|flatline.

Button ranks, the strip's fill, the glyph run and the type sizes (body 12, meta 11) are read from MainWindow.xaml, ControlStyles.xaml and GamesPage.xaml. The interactive edge is derived at runtime in the app; brand's value is exact, the other themes' are close estimates.

1. The line, five states

One strip (row 3), one notice tall. Order when several are true: contested, outage, known issue, drift, update. Below, each state alone as it is drawn when it is the top notice, at the default width (900) and at 380 px (compact). Tone split A1 is used here.

Outage wording, the six levels

2. "+2 more", collapsed and expanded

Collapsed, the area is one notice tall whatever the count. Expanded, each hidden notice is drawn with the same template, under the top one, with its own actions. Drawn with B1 (the plan default toggle). Here the top notice is the outage, with the known issue and the update behind it.

Decision A. Tone of the line

A1 splits by weight: contested, outage and drift in the warning colour, known issue and update in white. A2 puts all five in the warning colour. The glyph is the same on all five either way, so colour is never the only signal.

The two pairs this decision rides on, measured here

Text on the strip's own fill (RowBg), WCAG 2.x ratio against AA 4.5. These are the two named pairs spec §7.7 asks the build to measure; this table is the mock's own arithmetic from the theme values above, not the test's.

Decision B. The "+N more" control

B1 is the account panels' expander rank: open is a cyan outline and cyan text, never a fill. B2 is the shared secondary toggle: open is the grey press-sheen fill. Rest, hover and open of each, then each in place on the strip (collapsed and expanded).

Decisions C, D, E. The pulse in the Library and the panel

The count is "81,827 playing" (the existing Library_Playing string), the date "updated 2 days ago", joined by the existing {0} · {1}. It sits beside the "last played" line and never in its place. Games and servers here are made up.

C. The grid tile, three options

The current tile is 144 px wide with 8 px of padding, 128 px of text. Heights drawn: C1 276 (two meta lines added to the 246 tile), C2 276 (the joined line wraps to two lines), C3 261 (one added line). The build measures the real sizes; these are the spec's estimate of a 15 px meta line.

Pulse states, drawn in C1

D. The list row: the joined line

Drawn as a second line under the name, in the name column, at meta size. The row grows by one meta line. The columns to its right are v1.40's widths.

D. The panel entry: the joined line

The pulse is one more part of the entry's meta line, joined with the same " · ". At 400 px it fits; at 320 px it trims with an ellipsis and the full text lives in the tooltip and the accessible name, as the panel entry already works today.

E. A private server: the date, no count

Never a count, because the universe's number is not this server's. Shown in the tile (C1), the row and the panel entry above; here the three side by side.

Decision F. The update notice at 380 px

F is drawn as proposed: at compact the resolver always picks the short sentence and drops the batch clause. The long sentence at 380 px is drawn beneath for comparison only; it is not an option on the table.
